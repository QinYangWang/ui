Why Tessel

An agent you can hand real work to.

Autocomplete finishes your line. Tessel finishes the ticket, with a plan you can read and a diff you can trust.

Plans before it types

Every task starts as a checklist you can edit. Tessel works through it step by step and shows its progress live.

Runs in a sealed sandbox

Each session gets a fresh container with your repo, secrets scoped per project and no network unless you allow it.

Reviews its own diffs

A second pass reads the change like a senior reviewer: types, edge cases, tests that should exist and don't.

Asks before it acts

Pushing, deleting or calling paid APIs waits for your approval. Allow once, always, or deny with a note.

Fixes what CI flags

Hook it to your pipeline and Tessel picks up failing checks, reproduces them and pushes a fix to the same branch.

Shared with your team

Sessions, plans and approvals live in one workspace with SSO, audit logs and per-repo permissions.
